Lasagna

Meat Sauce

  • 1 lb (450g) ground beef (or half beef, half Italian sausage)
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 can (15 oz) tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tsp sugar (optional, reduces acidity)
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Salt & pepper to taste

In a large pan, cook the ground beef with the diced onion until browned. Add garlic and cook 1 minute. Stir in crushed tomatoes, tomato sauce, tomato paste, sugar, basil, oregano, salt, and pepper. Simmer 20–30 minutes (longer = richer flavor).

Cheese Filling

  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan
  • 1 tbsp chopped fresh parsley (or 1 tsp dried)
  • Salt & pepper

Mix ricotta, egg, Parmesan, parsley, salt, and pepper in a bowl. Set aside.

Assembly

In a 9×13 baking dish: Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom. Add a layer of noodles. Spread ⅓ of the ricotta mixture. Sprinkle ⅓ of the mozzarella. Add more meat sauce.

Bake

Cover with foil (tent it so it doesn’t touch the cheese). Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake another 20–25 minutes until bubbly and golden. Rest 10–15 minutes before cutting so it sets.
